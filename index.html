Features

Everything your business needs

From daily cash tracking to multi-branch management — CashBit handles it all in one simple app.

Point of Sale

Add products, scan barcodes, and process sales lightning fast. Supports multiple pricing units and categories.

Cash Book

Log cash in and out with notes. See your running balance at a glance with detailed transaction history.

Inventory Tracking

Monitor stock levels across products. Get alerts before you run out. Supports retail, wholesale, and manufacturing.

Multi-Branch

Register multiple branches and manage them all from one account. Switch between branches instantly.

Reports & Analytics

See sales trends, top products, and cash flow by day, week, or month. Know your business inside out.

Team Management

Add staff with role-based access. Track who made each transaction. Lock the register when stepping away.
